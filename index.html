MY PROJECTS

Below are my personal projects where I applied my knowledge and skill set I have acquired into.

Trade Reconciliation

In this project, I use SQL to reconcile 402 equity trades between a firm's internal records and its custodian's statement. Matching with price and amount tolerances reached a 91.5% auto-match rate and surfaced 37 breaks with $3.9M of open exposure. I classified each break, ranked them for escalation, and traced 43% of them to a single counterparty, then recommended same-day trade confirmation and daily reconciliation.

M&A Pipeline & Advisory Fee Model

In this project, I built an Excel model that tracks 52 live M&A deals ($650.7B in total value), estimates advisory fees with a tiered fee schedule and stage-based closing probabilities, and compares projected fee revenue against a quarterly budget. Automated data checks and a formula-driven one-page weekly report keep the pipeline review fast, and an interactive Tableau dashboard visualizes the results.

Customer Friction Analysis: Perpay & BNPL Peers

In this project, I analyze 31,252 CFPB consumer complaints about Perpay, Affirm, Klarna, Sezzle, and Self Financial to find where customers get stuck. I cleaned the data with Power Query, analyzed it with pivot tables, and built a Power BI dashboard with DAX. Incorrect credit-report information is the #1 complaint industry-wide (28%) and even higher for Perpay (34%), which led to recommendations for the Product, Merchandising, and Customer Service teams.
